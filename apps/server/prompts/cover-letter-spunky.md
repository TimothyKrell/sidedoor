## Style for this letter: spunky
Same person, same truth, more mischief. Keep the episode from the first draft. Sentence case, cheekier and more confident than a normal letter: someone who's been extremely online for years and isn't hiding it. Dry, playful, a little unserious about the form and completely serious about the work. A wink or a joke is welcome, opening included, and this overrides "Plain words" and the humor limit. For a crypto company one or two scene words can appear, where they're the natural word. For anyone else, none. It ends with plain thanks, no bit on it.

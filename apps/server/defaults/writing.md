# How I write

Agents read this before drafting anything you'll send: outreach, cover letters, application
answers, resume lines. Edit it so it sounds like you. Facts always come from your Experience
document; this page is only about how to say them.

## Voice
- Warm, plain, short, specific. Write the way you'd talk to a smart colleague.
- Lead with the most relevant thing you've done, not with enthusiasm about the company.
- One concrete example beats three adjectives. Numbers beat claims.
- End with a clear, small ask or next step.

## Things that make writing read as AI-generated (avoid them)
- **"Not just X, it's Y"** and "more than just". Say Y.
- **Everything in threes.** Three adjectives, three bullets, three short fragments in a row. Use two,
  or four, or one good one.
- **Em dashes and colon reveals** ("Here's the thing: ..."). Use a period.
- **Hype words:** passionate, thrilled, excited to, leverage, delve, robust, seamless, cutting-edge,
  game-changer, synergy, spearhead, tapestry, landscape, journey, unlock, elevate, empower.
- **Empty openers and closers:** "I hope this finds you well", "I'd love to connect!", "Looking
  forward to hearing from you!"
- **Fake causality:** "because" or "which means" connecting things that aren't really connected.
- **Adverbs doing the work:** really, truly, deeply, incredibly, genuinely.
- **Too-tidy structure:** every paragraph the same length, every bullet the same shape.
- **Exclamation points.** At most one per piece.

## Resume lines
- Start with a strong verb, say what you did and what changed because of it.
- Put the number where the reader will see it.
- One idea per bullet. Two lines at most.

## Editing
- When editing my own words, change as little as possible.
- Never add a fact, number, title or skill that isn't in my Experience document.
